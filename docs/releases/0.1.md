## FM1_Locus 0.1

The first release: a modal resonator played into a granular processor, on the M-VAVE FM-1.

- **RESONATOR**: modal, sympathetic strings, string, FM voice and quantized "chords" models, 1, 2 or 4 voices,
  struck by its own exciter, by the routed audio, or by the audio's own onsets.
- **LOCUS**: granular, stretch, looper and spectral modes with the full buffer (about 1 to 8 s), freeze on REC.
- **ROUTING**: the computer's USB audio into the resonator, into LOCUS and to the outputs; the resonator into LOCUS
  and to the outputs; LOCUS to the outputs. Each off to +6 dB.
- **SEQUENCER**: 8 patterns of 1 to 32 steps; each step a note, an accent, and optionally a grain.
- **CHANCE**: Geiger counter, Lorenz attractor, cellular automaton (rules 30, 90, 110) and dripping faucet triggers,
  for the resonator, the grains, or both.
- **GLO**: the visualizer, then the global settings (MIDI channel, keys fire grains, octave, CPU).
- USB audio both ways ("LOCUS FM-1"), MIDI notes, start / stop and program change in; the settings and patterns
  saved in flash.

The DSP is Emilie Gillet's Rings and Clouds code (MIT). Not yet tested on an FM-1: it runs in the FM-1 emulator,
where both cores together use about 40% of the audio interrupt's time in the heaviest settings. It has the update
loader of the other Felucca-platform firmware, so it installs and is replaced the same way (checked by the update
tests, including the return to M-VAVE's V15 and V16; not yet on a device).
